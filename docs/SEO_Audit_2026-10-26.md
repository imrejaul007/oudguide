## 2. The five pillars

### 2.1 Schema markup — **what we have**

Single source: `apps/web/src/lib/jsonld.ts`. Eight builders, all claim-anchored
to visible page content (a schema claim must appear on the page — enforced by
comment and by `tests/jsonld.test.ts`):

| Type | Where emitted |
|---|---|
| `Article` | `/guides/[...slug]`, `/products/[slug]` (plus index hubs) |
| `DefinedTerm` | `/glossary/[slug]` (replaces Article — correct) |
| `ItemList` | guides / glossary / products index pages, nested |
| `BreadcrumbList` | all of the above + directory profiles |
| `Dataset` | the two original-data pages (`quality/grade-label-dataset`, `quality/gulf-price-baseline`) — **verified emitting `Article + Dataset`** |
| `Organization` | home + directory profile pages (deliberately not `LocalBusiness` — the pages publish no address) |
| `WebSite` + `SearchAction` | home (EN-only, correctly gated) |
| `FAQPage` | directory profiles only |

**What's missing**
- **No `Product` schema — deliberately not added (correction, 2026-10-27).**
  This audit recommended a `Product` type on the 25 product-family pages.
  On re-reading the renderer, each family page carries a documented
  editorial decision: *"A family page explains a FORMAT … It is not a
  product page. It carries no price, no composition and no grade"*
  (`products/[slug]/page.tsx:22–34`). Emitting `Product` there would
  claim exactly what the page states it does not — the defect class the
  schema module's header forbids ("a claim in the schema must be visible
  on the page"). The honest `Product`/`Dealer` surface is the B2B
  supplier-registered listings, still WIP. Revisit when supplier product
  listings ship.
- **No `Person` author** — author/publisher are hard-coded to Organization
  "OudGuide" (`jsonld.ts:158–168`). Zero named human in the corpus (see 2.4).
- **No schema on the directory index** — the site's main commercial surface
  emits nothing while every child profile does. *Fix: `ItemList` of the
  rendered records + `BreadcrumbList`, mirroring the glossary index.*
- **No schema on `/rfq`, `/sourcing`, `/membership`, `/shortlist`, policy
  pages** — policy pages: appropriate. The service pages could take a
  `WebPage`/`Service` claim; low priority.
- **`FAQPage` is under-used** — guide `directAnswer` fields are prime FAQ
  material; add `FAQPage` to the high-question guides (fraud, CITES, GC-MS,
  grade labels, price baseline). Feeds answer engines even though Google's
  classic FAQ rich-result box is currently restricted.
- No `HowTo`, no `LocalBusiness`, no B2B/`Dealer` schemas (B2B pages are WIP
  and noindexed — revisit when that ships).

### 2.2 Internal linking — **what we have**

- **Zero orphans** — `tests/link-reachability.test.ts` passes 6/6. The three
  formerly-orphan clusters (36 destination guides, 40 origin regions,
  13 origin countries + 5 comparisons) were wired into hub `children` and
  per-page `related` arrays this cycle (commits `4b4c6c5`, `b15fd1c`).
- Glossary hub links all 35 terms as children; product families carry
  supplier-register panels and region children; the two new glossary
  disambiguations and the bakhoor-forms page are hub children.

**What's missing**
- **Lateral hub-to-hub links: 0.** 11 of 14 hubs have no `related[]` at all;
  the 3 that do point at sibling hubs only incidentally. The 14 silos don't
  cross-reinforce, which weakens the topical-cluster signal (§2.3). *Fix:
  add 1–2 sibling-hub edges per hub (quality↔sourcing↔trade, fragrance↔
  using-oud↔culture) — a data-only change in `hubs.ts`, gated by
  `content-integrity.test.ts`.*
- **Glossary terms are not inlined in guide prose.** `ArticleSection.body` is a
  plain `string[]` (`types.ts:64`), so 0 of the ~40 trade-term mentions in
  guide bodies link to `/glossary/<term>`. Worse: only **7 of 35** glossary
  terms are even *mentioned* in guide bodies — a topical-coverage gap, not
  just a link gap. *Fix (bigger, P3): a link-capable body type
  (`string | {term}`) + a controlled mention scanner; or a per-guide
  "related terms" block, which is the data-only approximation.*
- ~~**Header exposes 4 pillars; 3 are footer-only.**~~ **CORRECTED
  (2026-10-27): already satisfied.** `getPrimaryNav` (`lib/constants.ts:255`)
  returns seven items — Encyclopedia, Sourcing, Origins, Products, Glossary,
  For Business, Partner Portal — and the Header renders that array on both
  desktop (`Header.tsx:143`) and the mobile drawer (`Header.tsx:259`).
  Glossary, Products and the territory register are *not* footer-only. No
  change required.

### 2.3 Topical authority — **what we have**

The strongest asset. 236 articles organized as 14 hubs across six silos, a
shared 18-entry source registry (15 in active use), and two first-party
datasets no competitor has. The coverage matrix
(`docs/keyword-research/COVERAGE_MATRIX.md`) shows knowledge depth is
broadly owned; the gaps are commercial (vendor list), not educational.

**What's missing**
- **The 551-route unearned surface.** 772 manifest routes vs 221 served:
  30 editorial-guide routes have no article, 249 territory pages are
  indexable-but-unregistered shells (see §3 T-1), and 537 directory facets
  resolve to one supplier. The sitemap is *honest* (no 404s promised), but
  every unserved route is a keyword with zero on-page asset. Prioritize the
  30 editorial guides — they are real content, not shells.
- **Thin pillars.** Six hubs are sub-300-word: `collecting` 212, `culture`
  224, `using-oud` 231, `production` 248, `sourcing` 290, `trade` 290.
  Reference-tier has no word floor, but a pillar page this thin reads as
  "thin" to both crawlers and users. *Fix: deepen each with a
  "what this section is" scope + its most-cited sub-topics (content work).*
- **No `llms.txt`** — no AI-answer-channel surfacing of the hub→dataset
  structure (cheap, high-yield given the Dataset schemas that already exist).
- **No decision hub** ("which oud product do I actually want") — the
  buying-intent root that would consolidate all 24 product families
  (Tier-1 #3 in the coverage matrix, still unbuilt).

### 2.4 E-E-A-T — **what we have**

This is a real differentiator and it is mostly in place:
- **Experience** — two dated, first-party datasets with the "documented range,
  not a point" voice; RFQ market-data capture engine built (admin-gated).
- **Authoritativeness** — every published article cites ≥2 registry sources
  with ≥1 primary/regulatory (`tests/source-breadth.test.ts`), regulatory
  spine: CITES/ITTO/TRAFFIC/IFRA/FAO/CBIC.
- **Trust** — 11 policy pages that state what the site has *not* done
  (no fabricated field visits, no sponsored rankings, verification ≠
  payment, undisclosed commercial relationship disclosed). The About page
  honestly says the legal controller is not yet named.

**What's missing**
- **No named human author/reviewer.** `reviewer` = "OudGuide editorial
  review" on all 236 articles; the schema author is the organization. The
  missing signal is **Expertise + Accountability**. *Fix (user decision #1
  from the repair instructions): supply a real name + role; then add a
  `Person` with `sameAs` to `articleSchema`, a "Reviewed by" block on About /
  editorial-policy, and a credentials page.*
- **The Arabic tree is fully noindexed** (`ARABIC_HUMAN_REVIEW_COMPLETE
  = false`, `articles.ts:267`). The 11 Arabic data files are written and the
  encoding guard passes; a human review pass + flag flip is the entire
  structural blocker for every Arabic SERP (سعر عود, عود بالجملة,
  تصريح سايتس, …). *This is the single highest-impact item on the Arabic
  side.*

### 2.5 Original data / research — **what we have**

- **Two original datasets**, both emitting `Dataset` JSON-LD:
  `quality/grade-label-dataset` (4 variables) and `quality/gulf-price-baseline`
  (7 variables, commit `a02b690`) — the #1 Arabic commercial keyword asset.
- **RFQ market-data capture** (`packages/domain` `captureMarketDataPoint`,
  PII-stripped, k-anonymized aggregate at k=5) — the engine that turns the
  static baseline into a living one.
- A dated, cited source registry as a *single file* (`sources.ts`, 18
  entries incl. the newly verified CBIC personal-carriage instruction).

**What's missing**
- **The vendor list (the #1 commercial gap).** 3 seed businesses, 1 real
  (Al Mutalib Perfumes). 537 directory filter facets and every product
  family's supplier panel point at that one record or an empty set.
  *Fix: register 5–10 real, owner-verified suppliers — this unlocks the 537
  facet routes and is the site's B2B moat.*
- **Market capture is admin-only** (`admin/inquiries/page.tsx:22` is the sole
  consumer). No public surface. *Fix: once the B2B/lead-store WIP persists,
  show a k-anonymized, dated aggregate on `quality/gulf-price-baseline` and
  update its `Dataset.dateModified`.*
- **Bakhoor vendors = 0** — the new forms page's natural companion (a
  registered bakhoor maker) doesn't exist yet; covered by the supplier
  registration task.

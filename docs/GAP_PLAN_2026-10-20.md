# OudGuide Gap Plan

**Date:** 20 October 2026
**Inputs:** `docs/AUDIT_AGARWOODGUIDE_2026-10-20.md`, `docs/LIVE_SITEMAP_STATUS_2026-10-20.csv` (775 rows), `docs/AGARWOODGUIDE_CRAWL_2026-10-20.csv` (84 rows)
**Status:** Planning. No code changed by this document.

---

## The situation in one paragraph

The legacy WordPress site agarwoodguide.com has genuinely good editorial content (48 articles, verified citations, real business listings) wrapped in a technically broken shell (6 homepage 404s, an empty directory, 13 duplicate region archives, an orphaned business directory). The current oudguide.com deployment has the shell without the content: 570 of 775 sitemap URLs return 404, all 193 guide pages render the same hardcoded article, and no page has its own title, description, canonical, or schema. **The two properties we actually have are in the wrong place.** The fastest route to a real product is to move the legacy editorial asset onto the OudGuide shell, and to fix the shell's structural bugs first because they are cheap and they gate everything else.

---

## Sequencing principle

Fix the shell before the content. A sitemap that lies, and pages with no metadata, make every piece of editorial work harder to see. The shell fixes are days of work and remove the 570-URL 404 surface. The content work is months and depends on a per-page metadata system and a content schema that do not exist yet.

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Publishing original prose at 193-URL scale will outrun editorial capacity | Publish in waves. Keep unpublished guides out of the sitemap. A hub with 8 good articles beats a hub with 48 stubs. |
| The legacy site's content is strong enough to be tempting to copy | `EDITORIAL_RUNBOOK.md` §1.3 already prohibits it. Treat the legacy site as a citation map and a topic map only. |
| Adding routes to satisfy the sitemap re-creates thin content at scale | Route coverage is a Phase 0 fix (remove from sitemap) and a Phase 3 fix (build real pages). Do not build a route that renders a template. |
| The 249 territory pages are the biggest chunk of 404s and the thinnest content | Implement the route fix now; build genuine territory content slowly. Do not ship 249 near-identical pages — that is the legacy site's mistake. |
| Arabic parity doubles the content load | Ship English first, keep the Arabic tree out of the sitemap until it is reviewed, and add `hreflang` only for pairs that exist. |

# OudGuide EN Keyword Universe — Page-Planning Catalog

> Captured 2026-10-26 from the English-market keyword research agent (40+ live SERP
> probes). It already cross-checked our existing `seo-gap-pages.ts` (fraud, oud oil vs
> attar, freshness/moisture, CITES-by-market, GC-MS reading, price framework, attar
> evaluation, fine-fragrance market, which-species, grade-label dataset) and flags where
> new work overlaps. Prices and CITES figures are SERP-observed; the site's own rule is to
> publish them as dated, sourced bands, never flat claims.

## Clusters

### 1. What is oud / agarwood (definition + naming web) — HIGH
- Phrases: what is oud, what is agarwood, oud vs oudh, oud vs agarwood, agarwood vs aoud, gaharu meaning, eaglewood, "is oud the musical instrument", what does oud smell like
- Intent: informational, top-of-funnel; global
- SERP: Wikipedia + 2–3 brand blogs; weak on data (no species→region→scent→price table; terminology fragments; no oud-vs-instrument disambiguation)
- Needs: formation mechanism, full alias table with etymology, scent descriptor, links out to every other cluster; a disambiguation block for the instrument
- Vendor list: no (a "explore by species" nav instead)

### 2. Oud oil / attar / dehn al oud — HIGH
- Phrases: oud oil, what is attar, oud attar, dehn al oud, dehn al oud vs oud oil, pure oud oil, oud oil 100%, pasham
- Intent: informational + retail
- SERP: vendor blogs (Kannauj Attar, Amir Oud, Indicana), eBay/Amazon price noise; pure-oil vs sandalwood-base attar vs "dehn al oud" is genuinely confused
- Needs: direct answer (oud oil = distilled resin; attar = co-distilled into sandalwood; dehn al oud = the oil), yield ~0.1–0.5% of wood weight, GC-MS markers to demand, price per tola by origin
- Vendor list: yes — certified distillers
- Ours: extend "Oud oil vs attar" into dehn al oud glossary territory

### 3. Oud pricing (per kg / tola / ml by origin) — HIGH (flagship data gap)
- Phrases: assam oud price per kg, agarwood price per tola, oud oil price, why is oud so expensive, oud price cambodia vs india, price of laotian oud, is oud more expensive than gold
- Intent: informational + B2B signal
- SERP: Fatma "2026 price guide", oudoiltrading.com per-tola tables, IndiaMART, YouTube — every source gives different numbers, none is consistent, none shows a methodology
- Needs: concrete price bands by origin/grade with dates + stated assumptions, gold/price comparison, "why prices vary" (resin density, age, wild vs plantation)
- Vendor list: no (conflict of interest) — a sourcing-methodology section
- Ours: build the actual published price dataset (species × grade, dates, CITES status) in/around "A buyer's framework for oud oil price"

### 4. Origin species pages — HIGH (Assam/Cambodian/Laotian/Vietnamese), MED (Sumatran/Malaysian/qinam/kyara)
- Phrases: assam oud, hindi oud, cambodian oud, laotian oud, vietnamese oud, sumatran oud, kashmiri oud (NOT a species — a Mughal accord), qinan, kynam, kyara
- Intent: informational + commercial
- SERP: Première Peau strongest (per-origin glossary + 2025 wholesale prices); nobody has a systematic species dataset (A. crassna / A. agallocha / A. sinensis / Gyrinops) with scent, regions, legal status, price bands
- Needs: species, key regions, scent facets (Assam = animalic/leathery; Cambodian = sweet/fruity; Laotian = honeyed; Sumatran = earthy/damp; Seufi = "cool" opening; qinam = sweet/camphoric), price band, CITES status; Kashmiri needs a disambiguation (accord ≠ species)
- Vendor list: yes per origin (e.g. "Assam distillers")
- Ours: EXISTS (14 countries + 40 regions) — add Kashmiri/murruki disambiguation

### 5. Oud grades (VIP/Seufi/sinking/Kyara) — HIGH (data moat)
- Phrases: oud grade, VIP oud, seufi grade oud, sinking grade, kyara, oud grades explained, super grade agarwood
- Intent: informational + B2B
- SERP: vendor definitions, ResearchGate "Keeping Up Appearances" paper, ouddict; nobody publishes a grade-label→price-correlation dataset
- Needs: the vocabulary (floating/half-sink/sinking, A/B/C, Seufi, Kyara), what each implies for price, which labels are seller inventions vs trade standards
- Vendor list: no
- Ours: EXISTS ("Gulf-market oud grading vocabulary" + "The OudGuide grade-label dataset" + "Super/Double Super/Triple Super")

### 6. Real vs fake (wood + oil) — HIGH (no independent data-backed page exists)
- Phrases: how to tell if oud is real, fake oud oil, how to spot fake agarwood, is my oud oil genuine, polished vs unpolished chips, oud purity test, how to test oud oil at home
- Intent: informational + protective commercial; strong anti-fraud motivation
- SERP: vendor "authenticity" posts (all vested), ouddict/Reddit, our own "Oud fraud and counterfeit identification" — build it
- Needs: test battery (visual resin veins, burn/bubble, blotter, viscosity, longevity), GC-MS interpretation (link lab-report page), red-flag price thresholds, "what a reputable seller should provide"
- Vendor list: yes — "suppliers who publish GC-MS + origin paperwork"
- Ours: EXISTS (fraud + GC-MS + freshness + attar eval) — the defensible chain

### 7. CITES / import rules (US, EU, UAE, personal effects) — HIGH
- Phrases: import agarwood CITES, CITES permit agarwood, import oud oil USA, EU CITES agarwood, personal limits agarwood traveler, is oud illegal to import, CITES appendix II aquilaria, import bakhoor USA
- Intent: informational / B2B compliance; weak SERPs
- Facts that must be citable: all Aquilaria + Gyrinops Appendix II (A. malaccensis 1995, genera 2004); EU Annex B needs an EU import permit for commercial (export permit alone insufficient, Reg 338/97); personal-effects Res. 13.7 Rev.17 (1 kg chips, 24 ml oil, 2 bead sets, accompanied baggage); US via USFWS 50 CFR 23 + USDA-APHIS + FDA cosmetic claims; India plantation quota (~151,080 kg chips / 7,050 kg oil, 2024–27)
- Needs: per-market rule tables (US/EU/UAE/GCC/UK/India), product-type exemptions (Annotation 14: finished retail cosmetics exempt; raw chips/beads/bulk oil NOT), permit checklist, "what you can carry vs ship"
- Vendor list: no — a "certified import-ready suppliers" CTA
- Ours: EXISTS ("CITES import by market: US, UAE and EU") — expand to more markets + personal effects

### 8. Wholesale / B2B sourcing — HIGH for head term

- Intent: B2B

- Vendor list: YES — the supplier directory IS the answer

## Flags

**(a) Highest-value gaps (nobody has a data-backed answer):**
1. Pricing dataset (cluster 3) — no independent, method-stated, dated price-per-kg/tola by species×grade. Our "buyer's framework" is the home.
2. Grade-label dataset (cluster 5) — sell-through labels are seller inventions; no neutral mapping. B2B buyers need it most.
3. Independent authenticity protocol (cluster 6) — every "real vs fake" page is vendor-written. Ours + GC-MS + "publishes GC-MS" vendor list = complete defensible chain.
4. Citations-first benefits/claims (cluster 17) — PubMed-studied effects vs folklore, named papers.

**(b) High-volume terms where competitors are weak:**
- "bakhoor" head + format terms (cluster 10) — SERP is brand blogs; independent definition + vendor list wins.
- "agarwood wholesale supplier" (cluster 8) — unverified B2B directories; our verified directory + RFQ is the answer.
- "CITES import agarwood" (cluster 7) — forum threads and PDFs; our per-market page is unmatched.
- "best oud perfume" natural-vs-synthetic data angle (cluster 14).

**(c) Intent-ambiguous terms (must disambiguate or we look sloppy):**
- "Kashmiri oud" — retail attar/accord searches vs zero botanical reality (accord ≠ species).
- "Pasham oud" — searches are brand products, not a material; treat as glossary.
- "Agarwood" vs "oud" vs "oudh" vs "gaharu" vs "aoud" — same material, different audiences; one canonical term per audience.
- "Oud" the instrument vs the resin — disambiguation block at the top of the head page.
- "Oud vs bakhoor vs incense vs beads vs attar" — retail buyers conflate all five families; a "which oud product do I actually want" decision hub doesn't exist anywhere.

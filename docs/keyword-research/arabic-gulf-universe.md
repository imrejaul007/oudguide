# OudGuide — Arabic/Gulf & South-Asian keyword universe (retail + B2B)

> Captured 2026-10-26 from the Arabic-market keyword research agent.
> 20 WebSearch runs on Arabic queries (+ Indonesian gaharu, + English attar).
> Prices in SAR unless noted. This is research/coverage planning, not content — each
> cluster lists the phrases, intent, audience, current SERP, what a ranking page must
> contain, and priority. The build decision (which to answer first) is separate.

Method note: prices below are observed in current SERPs; treat every figure as a dated,
sourced band (the site's own rule), never a flat claim.

## Clusters

### 1. Core concept — «ما هو العود» (informational, top of funnel)
- Phrases: ما هو العود | العود والخشب (wood vs oil) | العود شجرة الاسم العلمي | Aquilaria the tree | أين ينمو العود (where it grows) | العود الأصلي | العود الطبيعي | العود الصناعي (synthetic)
- Intent: informational. Audience: Saudi/UAE consumers, diaspora.
- SERP: ar.wikipedia #1 for the bare term; retailer blogs (qshwa, alseefoud, maisonouds), TikTok/YouTube short-form. Wikipedia beats all but is shallow on trade.
- Needs: direct answer (resin from infected Aquilaria, 15–20 species, origin regions), wood/oil/beads disambiguation, region map, glossary block that doubles as an internal-link hub.
- Priority: MED (existing article territory; the disambiguation page is a link hub the site can own).

### 2. Authenticity & fraud detection — «دهن العود الأصلي وكشف الغش» (HIGH — trust asset)
- Phrases: كيف اعرف دهن العود الأصلي (how to know real oud oil) | الفرق بين دهن العود الأصلي والمغشوش (original vs fake) | العود الطبيعي من الصناعي | العود المحسّن (enhanced oud) | دهن عود الأصلي من المزيف (distinguish the real dehn) | تخفيف دهن العود (dilution/cheating) | العود الطبيعي الطبيعي الصناعي (the 3-tier: natural / enhanced / synthetic) | علامة عود طبيعي (signs of natural wood)
- Intent: informational/trust → drives retail + B2B. Audience: both.
- SERP: YouTube (Dar Ateek Oman, TikTok), retailer blogs (sunaalteeb, aljowie, imuzhl, bohalteeb) — all merchant self-interest; no neutral reference.
- Needs: the 3-tier model (طبيعي 100% / طبيعي محسّن / صناعي), physical test protocol (smell cold vs on ember, two-sided chip, vein density), price floor that signals fraud, CITES/rarity citation. The site's signature "independent reference" slot — no one else fills it neutrally.
- Priority: HIGH.

### 3. Region/species comparisons — «الفرق بين أنواع العود» (HIGH commercial)
- Phrases: الفرق بين العود الكمبودي والهندي | عود أسامي vs عود هندي (Assam is the region; "Indian" is the umbrella — retailers treat them separately, confusion to exploit) | عود لاوني (Laotian, rare, most expensive tier; old Lao ~45,000 SAR/kg claim) | عود سومطرة (Sumatran) | العود المروكي vs الكمبودي («murruki» is a market name, NOT Morocco!) | الكشميري (Kashmiri = blend name, not a grower region — verify; retailers sell it as if a region) | عود فيتنامي | عود ماليزي | عود بورمي | عود كرماليتان/كلمنتان (Kalimantan = the Borneo name Gulf buyers search)
- Intent: informational-to-retail. Audience: consumers choosing grades.
- SERP: retailer blogs dominate every pair (sunaalteeb, oud-saudi, qshwa, albadar-oud, imuzhl) with thin, contradictory claims (one blog claims murruki comes from "Moroccan forests" — botanically wrong; Aquilaria does not grow in Morocco). SERP-confusion gap the site can own with correct botany + price.
- Needs: one canonical region page per name (Asam, Kanboud, Lāwī, Ṣumatra, Kalimatān, Fīnam, Mālayī, Burmese, «Marūki») — what it actually is (real region or trade name), scent profile, tier, price bands with sources, supplier list. Compare-and-contrast matrix at the hub.
- Priority: HIGH.

### 4. Retail purchase — «شراء العود أونلاين» (HIGH retail)
- Phrases: شراء العود أونلاين السعودية | عود طبيعي شراء أونلاين | شراء العود دبي | عود طبيعي فاخر شراء | عود بالقطعة شراء (per piece) | أين اشترى العود الأصلي | عود أونلاين توصيل | عود هدية شراء
- Intent: transactional/retail. Audience: Saudi/UAE consumers.
- SERP: Gulf retailers (arabianoud, ajmal, oudelite, saudioud, ksaoud, alsalman) — pure e-commerce, no reference content. The site wins on "how to buy without being scammed" (authenticity + independent supplier list + price benchmarks), which retailers cannot publish.
- Needs: buying guide (grade tiers, unit systems ʿūqiya/tola/thumman/kilo, price-floor table), region-matched supplier list, CITES/customs note, red flags.
- Priority: HIGH.

### 5. Oud oil / attar — «دهن العود / زيت العود / attar» (HIGH)
- Phrases: دهن العود الأصلي | دهن العود الهندي (Kannauj tradition) | زيت عود طبيعي | عطر العود زيت | attar عود | الفرق بين دهن العود الهندي والكمبودي | دهن العود الأصلي كيف تميز | التولة كم مل (the tola ≈ 12 ml / 11.66 g) | سعر التولة دهن العود | attar عود بدون كحول (alcohol-free)
- Intent: retail/informational. Audience: perfume consumers + perfumers.
- SERP: Gulf perfume houses (arabianoud, ajmal, abuzarifragrances) + Indian attar shops (kannaujattar) + YouTube "4 attars at 4 prices." Pure attar retail ~100–500 USD/tola; wholesale Kannauj >150,000 INR/kg claim.
- Needs: oil-vs-wood explainer, the tola unit, region price bands, authenticity tests, supplier list (Kannauj + Gulf).
- Priority: HIGH.

### 6. Bakhoor family — «بخور / مسقي / موشق / ممشك / تبر / دقة» (HIGH retail)
- Phrases: بخور عود شراء | الفرق بين المسقي والموشق والممشك (the #1 confusion — three bakhoor forms) | بخور مسقي (wood soaked in oil) | بخور موشق (waṣaq resin + orange blossom) | بخور ممشك / بخور مسك | شمع مسك (musk wax) | تبر عود (oud "sawdust" grade; search «تبر العود» ranks the Qarshi 70 g tin) | دقة عود (broken-wood grade) | المعمول عود (molded tablets) | مبخرة بخور (the burner device)
- Intent: retail. Audience: Gulf households.
- SERP: thin — one page (time-altayyib) explains masqī; the masqī/mūshiq/mumshak distinction is scattered and confused even among merchants. No canonical Arabic page.
- Needs: the three-form explainer (what's in each, how to use, price), burner/charcoal basics, region supplier list.
- Priority: HIGH (big SERP gap).

### 7. Musk & attar/fragrance-adjacent — «مطيبات عطارة مسك» (MED–HIGH)
- Phrases: الفرق بين المسك الأبيض والأخضر والأسود | مسك الطهارة (white/ṭahāra) | مسك الغزال (deer musk — CITES-restricted; retail sites tout "black deer musk," flag it) | مطيبات | عطارة شراء | عطر مسك للبيت | الفرق بين العود والمسك
- Intent: retail/informational. Audience: consumers.
- SERP: Gulf perfume blogs + YouTube; merchants confuse "aswad" (black/deer) vs "ṭahāra" (white); deer musk is CITES-protected (flag; mirrors the site's no-contact-fields/legality care).
- Needs: the musk-type table (white/black/ṭahāra/gazāl, source, CITES status, price), link to the attar cluster.
- Priority: MED–HIGH.

### 8. Beads / tasbih — «سبحة عود / خرز عود» (LOW–MED, clean retail slot)
- Phrases: سبحة عود طبيعية | مسبح عود 99 حبة | خرز عود أصلي | سبحة عود كم سعر | سبحة عود بالجملة (wholesale beads) | عود السبحة/الصليبة (Egyptian market for beads — SERP skews Egyptian, not Gulf; whitespace)
- Intent: retail + wholesale. Audience: both.
- SERP: video ("سبحة خرز عود بالجمله"); the Egyptian SERP is thin on a Gulf-standard page.
- Needs: bead authenticity tests (real resin vs dyed wood), unit counts (33/99/100), price floor, suppliers.
- Priority: LOW–MED.

### 9. Price questions — «سعر العود للكيلو / التولة» (HIGH — highest B2B value)
- Phrases: سعر كيلو عود كمبودي | سعر عود المروكي كيلو | سعر التولة عود | سعر عود الهندي | الفرق في سعر كيلو عود | عود كمبودي سعر 2026 (year-tagged) | سعر عود بالدرهم الإمارات
- Observed SERP bands (cite in content, dated): kan'būdī ṭāqīr ~550–600 SAR/kg; kan'būdī malak natural ~18,000+ SAR/kg; murūki muḥaṣṣan 300–550/kg, natural 3,000–7,000/kg; hindi natural 8,000–13,000/kg; lāwī natural old 45,000/kg claim; attar >150,000 INR/kg; gaharu (IDR) 300k–20M/kg retail, super-king up to US$100k/kg.
- Intent: transactional-adjacent/informational; single highest-leverage page for B2B RFQ capture.
- SERP: merchant price lists (qshwa, gmlhaloud, alhamidioud) + Haraj marketplace posts — no neutral price-baseline reference. The site's signature B2B asset.
- Needs: per-region price table with date, band, source; ʿūqiya/tola/thumman/kilo conversion; "why your price might be scammed" floor; RFQ CTA.
- Priority: HIGH (top B2B intent).

### 10. Wholesale / B2B — «عود بالجملة / تجار / موردين» (HIGH)
- Phrases: شراء عود بالجملة | عود بالجملة للتجار | موردين العود بالجملة (wholesale suppliers) | عود بالجملة في جدة/الرياض/دبي | قسم الجملة (wholesale section) | تصفيات جملة | بيع العود بالجملة | الجملة من المصدر (from source) | استيراد العود بالجملة
- Intent: B2B. Audience: traders, new retailers, importers.
- SERP: khs-trading (Riyadh + Jeddah wholesale-guide blogs), nateqoud's wholesale section, jiblhoud, gmlhaloud — thin, merchant-owned. No neutral "who supplies what and where" directory.
- Needs: the supplier directory (site's core asset) with market map (Muʿayqaliyā in Riyadh, Mārīsh al-Dubay / Nāʾif / al-Subkhā in Dubai), volume pricing, RFQ CTA, logistics + CITES notes.
- Priority: HIGH (site's differentiator).

### 11. Import / CITES / customs — «استيراد العود / تصريح سايتس / جمارك» (HIGH, big gap)
- Phrases: استيراد العود إلى السعودية | تصريح سايتس العود (CITES permit for oud) | العود قانوني استيراد | كم عود مسموح استيراد شخصي (personal limit) | تصريح الجمارك | CITES-2 | العود المهرب (smuggling/contraband distinction) | شهادة منشأ عود (certificate of origin)
- Key facts to encode: Aquilaria + Gyrinops = CITES Appendix II; Saudi personal limit 1 kg wood/chips + 1 tola (~12 g) attar (ZATCA 26 Jun 2021); commercial imports need CITES export permit + commercial invoice + certificate of origin + SFDA registration.
- Intent: B2B/informational. Audience: importers.
- SERP: CITES PDFs, agarwood.net English blogs translated to Arabic, ajel news snippets — no authoritative Arabic "import oud to the Gulf: step by step."
- Needs: per-country import step-by-step (KSA, UAE, Egypt, Morocco), CITES permit sourcing, customs duties, red flags; a regulatory-trust asset and natural RFQ entry point.
- Priority: HIGH (gap).

### 12. Markets & cities — «سوق العود / المعقلية / مرشد» (MED, B2B + retail crossover)
- Phrases: سوق المعقلية عود (Riyadh's oud wholesale center, 210+ shops) | سوق مرشد عود (Dubai Dir'ah, named after Kuwaiti trader Mārish al-'Asīmi, mid-20th c.) | سوق نايف (Dubai perfumes) | محلات عود في جدة | سوق العود دبي | شارع السبخة عود
- Intent: B2B sourcing + tourism. Audience: traders visiting markets.
- SERP: khs-trading (Riyadh + Jeddah "best wholesale shops 2026"), alamialoud, dar-aloud1 — thin, merchant-owned.
- Needs: market guide (which market sells what, wholesale vs retail hours, how to negotiate) with a supplier overlay.
- Priority: MED.

### 13. Burners / usage — «مبخرة / كيف أستخدم العود» (MED–LOW)
- Phrases: مبخرة كهربائية الفرق | كيف أستخدم عود | مبخرة بخور ذكي (smart burner) | فحم بخور الفرق (charcoal) | الفرق في مبخرة كهربائية وعادية | مبخرة عود هدية | بخور للسيارة (car incense)
- Intent: informational/retail. Audience: households.
- SERP: oud-saudi electric-vs-traditional guide, techstationsa, retailer pages; usage thin, mostly "how to use charcoal."
- Needs: burner types + safe usage + charcoal selection + link to the bakhoor cluster.
- Priority: MED–LOW.

### 14. Gifting / occasions — «هدايا عود / رمضان / توزيعات» (MED, seasonal)
- Phrases: هدية عود | عود هدية فاخرة | عود هدية رمضان | عروض عود رمضان | مبخرة هدية | توزيعات عود (corporate/hospital gifts) | عود هدية يوم التأسيس (Saudi National Day; altieb's "Foundation Day pack" ranks) | عود هدية زواج (wedding)
- Intent: retail/transactional. Audience: Gulf households, corporate.

- Priority: MED.

### 15. Cultivation / farms — «زراعة العود / مزارع» (LOW–MED, B2B-adjacent)
- Phrases: زراعة شجرة عود | كم سنة عود يتولد (7–15 yr range) | مزارع عود | العود الاصلي محسن (cultivated vs natural) | كم كيلو عود من شجرة (yield 3–6 kg chips/tree) | كيف يتولد العود (fungal inoculation)
- Intent: informational/B2B-adjacent. Audience: farmers, B2B buyers.
- SERP: plantationinternational, tractor-karvan, NCBI paper, m-arabi blog, TikTok farm tours. No neutral Arabic explainer with farm + yield data.
- Needs: cultivation primer (inoculation, 7–15 yr, yield, cost, CITES on farm-grown) + farm-source supplier list.
- Priority: LOW–MED.

### 16. Gaharu (Indonesian/Malay) — «gaharu harga / gaharu asli» (MED, separate-language market)
- Phrases (transliterated): gaharu harga per kg | gaharu asli (real gaharu) | harga kayu gaharu asli | gaharu terbaik (best) | cara membedakan gaharu asli (how to tell real) | jual gaharu kalimantan | harga gaharu 2026
- Observed price (IDR): Rp300k–20M/kg retail (low→medium), Rp25M+ export grade, super-king US$100k/kg; gaharu = Borneo/Kalimantan + Sumatra origin, distinct market from "oud."
- Intent: retail/B2B, Indonesian. Audience: Indonesian + global (Dubai resellers price Kalimantan gaharu ~50,000 AED/kg per one source).
- SERP: CNN Indonesia, CNBC Indonesia, brighton.co.id, IDX channel, Lazada/Tokopedia. No Malay-language reference page (gaharu vs oud, grades, price, suppliers). A single page could capture it — cheap, high value.
- Priority: MED (separate market, quick win with the "independent reference + supplier list" template).

### 17. Oud perfume — «عطر عود» (MED–LOW, retail)
- Phrases: عطر عود للرجال | عطر عود للنساء | عود عطر ثبات (longevity) | الفرق بين عطر عود طبيعي (natural vs artificial) | عطارة مسك عود
- Intent: retail. Audience: consumers (Gulf + diaspora).
- SERP: vperfumes, feviumsa, fragrance arabia, jumia, dookhon, TikTok lists; retailers talk about Western perfumes with oud notes (Tom Ford Oud Wood, Lattafa), not natural-oud sourcing.
- Needs: "real oud oil attar vs oud note in commercial perfumes" (ties to authenticity cluster); perfumery-side supplier list.
- Priority: MED–LOW.

## Flags

### (a) Top-5 Arabic commercial keywords the site must own
1. **عود بالجملة / شراء عود بالجملة** (wholesale / wholesale buy) — highest B2B value; SERP is merchant-owned and thin; the site's supplier directory + RFQ is the only neutral play.
2. **سعر عود / سعر كيلو عود / سعر التولة** (per-kilo / per-tola price) — a dated price-baseline reference is the site's signature B2B asset; all current answers are merchant price lists.
3. **استيراد العود / تصريح سايتس** (import / CITES permit) — a regulatory-trust gap; no single Arabic step-by-step exists, and it gates every B2B deal.
4. **دهن العود الأصلي / كيف تميز** (real oud oil / how to tell) — the trust moat; YouTube + merchant blogs dominate, no neutral reference.
5. **شراء العود أونلاين / الفرق بين العود الأصلي** (buy oud online / original oud) — retail capture; merchants can't publish a "how to buy without being scammed" page; the site can.

### (b) Retail-vs-B2B intent mismatch
- Retail-intent, B2B-content gap: clusters 4 (buy online), 6 (bakhoor), 8 (beads), 13 (burners), 14 (gifting), 17 (perfume) are consumer searches; the site's current content (RFQ widget, supplier directory, wholesale pages) under-serves them. A consumer-facing "how to buy + supplier list + price benchmark" template per cluster bridges the two.
- B2B-intent, retail-content gap: clusters 9 (price), 10 (wholesale/suppliers), 11 (import/CITES) are trader queries; retail merchant sites dominate, and the site's B2B angle (neutral price baseline, CITES step-by-step, market map, direct-supplier RFQ) is the only winning content — but it must be discoverable for retail searchers too (publish the price table publicly; gate only the supplier contact per the assistant-privacy rule).

### (c) High-value Arabic questions where the current SERP is bad/absent
1. "Is it legal to import oud? What's the CITES permit process, KSA/UAE?" — SERP is CITES PDFs, news, translated English blogs. No Arabic step-by-step with a per-country table. Big gap, high trust value.
2. "What's the real price of [region] oud per kg / per tola, 2026?" — SERP is merchant price lists + one Haraj post. No dated, neutral price baseline. The site's data asset fits.
3. "How do I tell my oud / attar isn't fake?" — SERP is YouTube + merchant "spot the fake" posts (all selling). No neutral protocol.
4. "What's the difference between masqī, mūshiq, mumshak bakhoor?" — SERP is one thin page + scattered Instagram. No canonical explainer.
5. "Is 'murrūki oud' from Morocco? Why do Gulf traders call it that?" — SERP is contradictory (one blog claims "Moroccan forests," botanically wrong). The site can fix this trade-name confusion and own the region-name taxonomy — a rare content moat.

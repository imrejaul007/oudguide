# OudGuide.com — Public Website Audit and Developer Remediation

**Inspection date:** 20 October 2026.

## Verdict

The public build is a partial implementation, not ready for acceptance as the complete OudGuide portal. The clearest release blockers are repeated guide bodies, published broken destinations, contradictory contact/subscription rules, unsupported verification language, and demo-like opportunities labelled as live. This assessment concerns inspected public evidence, not an assertion that every backend feature is absent.

## Method and limits

This register contains 54 checked URL observations: 33 content-retrieved URLs (including language and query variants), one operator-login redirect, 12 published internal targets reported as 404, one malformed search-discovered URL reported as 404, three crawler endpoints inaccessible through the tool, three SVG references unsupported by the tool, and the intermittently inaccessible root. It is a sampled public-content and link audit, not an exhaustive rendered-browser crawl.

Text was obtained through the web research tool, whose results can reflect provider caches. No production inquiry, payment, account, correction request, or privacy request was submitted. No access control was bypassed. Raw head tags, JS interactions, mobile presentation, Core Web Vitals, server-side data projection, database persistence and third-party integrations were not verified. Robots and sitemap access failures are NOT findings that those files are missing.

## Comparison with the approved plan

The v4 master instruction requires distinct seller location, material origin and buyer destination; retail and wholesale coverage; contact restrictions; source-backed knowledge; a neutral OudGuide intake; and separately controlled paid membership and introductions. Its route register explicitly distinguishes candidates from published pages.

The original route record OG-0096 for Assam is a candidate with no finished article supplied. It requires original regional information and primary evidence. Its publication prerequisite resembles the check-like wording shown on the live page. The original Q05 field contains an internal privacy/interpretation note about unit conversion; that note is visible on the public RFQ. The inference is that planning metadata has been used too directly as public content. This is based on the supplied reference files and public rendering, not a review of application source code.

## Positive foundations observed

The public site exposes an English information structure and Arabic navigation, a guide index, country records, a three-result directory, a multi-stage RFQ interface, the intended annual headline price, commercial referral disclosure, privacy/correction interfaces, and an operator-login boundary. Those are useful starting points, but interfaces and labels are not end-to-end test evidence.

## Priority definitions

- **P0:** address before accepting the site as production-ready for scaled publication, paid membership or real supplier introductions.
- **P1:** core content, discovery, UX and reliability fixes before broad promotion.
- **P2:** presentation and measured optimization work; do not confuse untested with failed.

### OG-A01 — Replace shared generic guide bodies (P0)

**Evidence status:** observed.

**Observed:** Seven inspected English article/hub destinations repeat the same main botanical, distillation and trade sections despite different titles. Grade, sample-plan, origin and local-buying questions are not answered specifically.

**Required change:** Remove the fallback article from public templates. Store a unique reviewed body per published page. Keep incomplete candidates as drafts; use a real hub template for navigation.

**Acceptance:** Strip navigation/referrals and compare main-content fingerprints; identical bodies are flagged. Each released page passes a question-specific editorial review.

## Recommended repair order

1. Disable production demo data and unsupported public claims. Stop publishing generic fallback guides; protect unfinished content without blocking the whole useful site.
2. Repair route resolution, aliases and publication-aware linking. Preserve valid URLs, create equivalent redirects where warranted, and remove misleading links.
3. Correct membership/contact semantics and named-recipient controls. Test before paid activation or real lead dispatch.
4. Restore independent geography/product/role discovery and add researched organizations. Do not substitute fabricated seed suppliers for missing coverage.
5. Commission and review genuinely distinct knowledge pages. Repair Arabic content and purpose-built hub templates.
6. Complete raw HTML, sitemap, schema, accessibility, performance and operational acceptance testing.

## SEO and evidence guidance

Google's spam guidance addresses scaled unoriginal pages with little user value. The observed duplication is a risk requiring correction, not proof of a manual action or an automatic duplicate-content penalty. Google also documents the importance of meaningful link labels and distinct page titles. If pages are temporarily noindexed while being repaired, allow the crawler to fetch the noindex instruction; robots blocking is not a substitute.

Google's AI search guidance applies ordinary search fundamentals rather than a special AI markup trick. IFRA explains that it does not itself issue certificates or authorize certification on its behalf; conformity must be described for its actual scope and intended use. Do not treat a site-wide certification badge as product-specific evidence.

## Evidence still needed for full acceptance

Request the source/release identifier; implementation matrix; content publication register; role/tenant permission tests; form/OTP persistence tests; named-recipient dispatch tests; subscription and tax/provider setup; live-versus-demo controls; raw HTML/meta/schema/robots/sitemap export; original approved media records; browser screenshots and performance reports. ## Completion decision

Do not use the counts of planned routes, countries or form fields as proof of completed research or implementation.
